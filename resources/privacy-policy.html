Privacy Policy

How Kayo Pulse collects, protects, and manages personal and operational information across every engagement.

At Kayo Pulse, we are committed to protecting privacy and managing data responsibly across our platform, services, partnerships, and projects. This policy outlines how we collect, use, store, process, and safeguard personal data in line with applicable law and good practice.

Overview

Kayo Pulse Limited is committed to protecting personal data, safeguarding confidential information, and promoting the responsible use of data across its software, systems, programmes, services, and external engagements.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you interact with Kayo Pulse — whether through our platform, a software subscription, a pilot programme, project implementation, partnership, or any other authorised engagement.

This policy applies to all external parties engaging with Kayo Pulse. Where a separate Data Processing Agreement or programme-specific policy also applies, those documents should be read together with this policy. In case of inconsistency, applicable law and any signed agreement take precedence.

Scope

This policy covers:

  • All personal data processed by Kayo Pulse Limited — automated or non-automated, in any format, including printed documents, digital files, images, and audio recordings.
  • All Kayo Pulse operations within and outside Kenya in relation to data subjects located in Kenya.
  • All Kayo Pulse employees and third parties who engage with Kayo Pulse.

Data We Collect

Depending on how you engage with Kayo Pulse, we may collect the following types of information:

Personal Data

Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person — such as your name, email address, phone number, or organisational affiliation — that you provide when using our services, entering into agreements, or corresponding with us.

Sensitive Personal Data

Information classified as sensitive under applicable data protection laws, including data relating to health, biometric characteristics, genetic data, racial or ethnic origin, and property details. We collect and process such data only where a lawful basis exists and appropriate safeguards are in place.

Environmental & Geospatial Data

Information relating to environmental conditions — land cover, forest cover, biomass, vegetation, soil, water, biodiversity, greenhouse gas emissions — and information associated with a geographic location, including coordinates, boundaries, satellite imagery, GPS records, and plot boundaries. This data is core to Kayo Pulse's Earth observation and ESG platform services.

Confidential Information

Non-public business, commercial, financial, technical, operational, project, client, partner, or other information disclosed or made accessible through an engagement with Kayo Pulse.

Lawful Use of Your Data

Kayo Pulse processes personal data on one or more of the following lawful bases under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019:

  • Consent — where you have given clear, informed consent for a specific purpose.
  • Contractual necessity — where processing is required to perform a contract to which you are a party, or to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to process your data to comply with a legal or regulatory requirement.
  • Vital interests — where processing is necessary to protect the vital interests of you or another person.
  • Public interest or official authority — where processing is necessary for a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for our legitimate interests or those of a third party, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms.

Our Data Protection Principles

All information handling across Kayo Pulse systems and engagements observes the following principles:

Lawfulness & Transparency

Information is handled lawfully, fairly, and transparently, with appropriate notice provided to affected individuals.

Purpose Limitation

Data is collected and used only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes — never repurposed without a lawful basis.

Data Minimisation

Only information that is necessary for the relevant engagement or service is collected or shared.

Accuracy

Reasonable steps are taken to ensure information is accurate, complete, and updated where necessary.

Storage Limitation

Personal data is not retained longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Integrity & Confidentiality

Appropriate safeguards protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

Accountability

Kayo Pulse can demonstrate compliance with applicable data protection responsibilities at all times.

Cross-border Protection

Personal data transferred outside Kenya complies with the requirements of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Confidentiality & Intellectual Property

Information exchanged through Kayo Pulse engagements may contain confidential business, technical, financial, or project information. Kayo Pulse and all external parties with access to such information are required to:

  • Maintain the confidentiality of non-public information received through the engagement.
  • Use confidential information only for the purpose for which it was disclosed.
  • Not disclose confidential information to unauthorised persons or organisations.
  • Ensure that employees, consultants, and subcontractors with access to confidential information are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • Protect confidential information using reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.
  • Return or securely delete confidential information when required by the applicable agreement or upon termination of the engagement.

Nothing in this policy transfers ownership of intellectual property between Kayo Pulse and an external party. Ownership and permitted use of software, source code, platform architecture, databases, methodologies, reports, models, designs, and datasets are governed by applicable licences, contracts, NDAs, or other written arrangements.

Security

Kayo Pulse has implemented appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect personal data effectively. These include:

  • Access to personal data is limited to personnel who require it, with appropriate access controls in place to prevent unauthorised sharing.
  • Destruction or deletion of personal data — whether printed or electronic — is carried out so that records are rendered irrecoverable.
  • Appropriate backup and disaster recovery solutions are maintained.
  • Kayo Pulse employees are bound by Non-Disclosure Agreements. Knowingly or recklessly disclosing personal information to third parties is a disciplinary offence.
  • Third-party service providers are required to provide sufficient guarantees of data protection and must act only on instructions received from Kayo Pulse.

Kayo Pulse will undertake a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) whenever a processing operation is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects.

Data Retention, Return & Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected or processed, and subject to applicable contractual, operational, regulatory, and legal requirements.

Once the retention period has expired, or upon valid request, personal data will be securely deleted or returned as appropriate. Electronic records are deleted using methods that make recovery or retrieval practically impossible.

Sharing & International Transfers

Kayo Pulse does not sell personal data. We do not disclose personal data or confidential information to unauthorised third parties.

Information may be shared where necessary for the following purposes:

  • Delivering contracted services and operating Kayo Pulse systems.
  • Engaging authorised technology providers, consultants, and subcontractors.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations.
  • Supporting project monitoring, reporting, auditing, and verification.
  • Facilitating approved partnerships and programme implementation.
  • Responding to lawful requests from competent authorities.

Where personal data is shared with a third-party service provider, Kayo Pulse takes reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate contractual, technical, and organisational safeguards are in place.

Transfers Outside Kenya

Personal data is transferred outside Kenya only where:

  • The destination jurisdiction has commensurate data protection laws, or appropriate safeguards are in place; and
  • The transfer is necessary for the purpose for which the data was originally collected.

All cross-border transfers comply with the requirements of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and relevant regulations.

Your Rights

As a data subject, you have rights under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. These include the right to:

  • Access — ask whether we hold personal data about you and request a copy of that data and information about how it is processed.
  • Rectification — request that inaccurate or incomplete personal data be corrected.
  • Erasure — request deletion of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or where it has been processed unlawfully.
  • Restriction — request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Objection — object to the processing of your personal data, including for direct marketing purposes.
  • Portability — request the transfer of personal data you have provided to us, in a structured and commonly used format, where processing is based on consent or a contract.
  • Withdraw consent — where we process your data on the basis of consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time, and we will cease processing for that purpose.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@kayopulse.com.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how Kayo Pulse collects, processes, stores, discloses, or uses your personal data, you may submit a complaint to us directly.

We will acknowledge your complaint within 7 working days and investigate the matter within 30 working days. If additional time or information is required, we will notify you before the initial period expires. The outcome will be communicated to you in writing by email.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may request a review of the decision. You also have the right to lodge a complaint directly with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) through its official channels.

Data Breach Notification

In line with regulatory requirements, Kayo Pulse will report any data breach to the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Where the identity of affected data subjects can be established, Kayo Pulse will also communicate the breach to those individuals as soon as practicable.

AVOS Africa

Built for African skies. Engineered for global compliance.

As the aviation industry steps into CORSIA Phase 2, African carriers face a new era of mandatory emissions monitoring, strict verification standards, and complex cross-border reporting. Navigating these requirements shouldn’t require heavy in-house carbon accounting teams or fragmented spreadsheets.

AVOS-Africa is an intelligent, cloud-native compliance workspace designed specifically to streamline Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) for African airline operators. We bridge the gap between fragmented flight logs and rigorous international standards.

What Sets Us Apart

The Audit-Ready Layer: We don’t disrupt your existing core systems or flight dispatch tools. Instead, AVOS-Africa acts as a seamless, verifier-ready audit trail and secure workspace that cuts down verification friction.

Automated Data-Gap Tracking: Our engine continuously monitors fuel reconciliation and tracks the critical 5% data-gap cap monthly—preventing nasty surprises before audit deadlines.

Hyper-Local Alignment: Built in tandem with regional regulatory frameworks (including Kenya's CORSIA guidelines), we ensure complete alignment with local civil aviation authorities and accreditation bodies.

Informed by Global Best Practices: Inspired by modern digital traffic management architectures (such as advanced European environmental monitoring frameworks), we bring world-class data integrity and automated reporting to African aviation corridors.

Designed for operators

Whether you're a major flag carrier managing complex international fleets or a regional operator tracking the 10,000-tonne compliance threshold, AVOS-Africa takes the headache out of carbon reporting so you can focus on flying.

Regulatory alignment

By connecting flight operations, fuel use, and monthly verification windows, we help airlines stay compliant across CORSIA, local authority needs, and auditor expectations.
